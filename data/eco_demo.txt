ECO Number: ECO-DEMO-001
Title: Extension of Qualified Sterile Shelf Life for CardioGraft Vascular Sealant
Product Family: PF-CG-01 (CardioGraft Vascular Sealant)
Change Type: Labeling Specification Update
Change Category: Packaging and Shelf Life Extension
Originator: Dr. Elena Vance, Senior Packaging Systems Engineer
Regulatory Approval Ref: REG-2026-CG-00912
Effective Date: 2026-11-15
Target Markets: US (FDA 510(k) K240891), EU (MDR CE 712034)

1. CHANGE DESCRIPTION:
Based on completed real-time aging protocol (TR-2026-881) and accelerated stability
validation in accordance with ISO 11607-1:2019 and ASTM F1980-21, the qualified
sterile shelf life for all CardioGraft Vascular Sealant sterile packaging configurations
is extended from 24 months to 36 months.

2. SPECIFIC LABEL ATTRIBUTE CHANGES:
- Attribute: Baseline Sterile Shelf Life
  Previous Value: 24 Months
  Proposed Value: 36 Months
  Justification: Real-time aging test report TR-2026-881 demonstrates package integrity,
  seal strength >1.5 N/mm, and barrier efficacy at 36 months post-sterilization (EO).

- Attribute: Storage Conditions
  Previous Value: Store between 2°C and 8°C. Do not freeze.
  Proposed Value: Store between 2°C and 8°C. Do not freeze.
  Justification: Preserved without modification.

- Attribute: Sterilization Method
  Current Value: Ethylene Oxide (EO)
  Proposed Value: Ethylene Oxide (EO)
  Justification: Preserved.

3. SCOPE AND APPLICABILITY:
This change applies to all primary sterile pouches and secondary shelf cartons
manufactured for the CardioGraft Vascular Sealant product family (PF-CG-01).
Tertiary shipping containers and standalone unsterilized literature (IFUs) that
do not carry sterile expiration dates are excluded from shelf-life label updates.
